The plot of William Shakespeare's 'A Midsummer Night's Dream'

View on Oak Academy

I can explain the plot and main themes of Shakespeare’s ‘A Midsummer Night’s Dream’.

Key learning points
  1. ‘A Midsummer Night’s Dream’ is a Shakespearean comedy.
  2. There are three sets of characters in the play.
  3. The play starts and ends in Athens; in the middle, the play is set in a magical forest.
  4. The play ends with a number of marriages.
  5. A summary of the plot helps us understand some of the play’s main themes.

WhyAll of Shakespeare's plays are divided into acts. These acts are divided into scenes. A change of act or scene can signal a change in location, mood or time.
3All of Shakespeare's plays are divided into acts. How many acts is each divided into?
Hint
Consider that there is an act for the climax of the play, and an act for the resolution of the play. How many additional acts might be needed to establish characters, and to allow the plot to develop?
Show answer
Answer5
WhyThe five acts usually follow this structure: exposition (establishing characters), rising action, climax, falling action, resolution.

WhyShakespeare's plays are (roughly) divided into comedies, tragedies and histories. Some people define some of his plays as 'problem plays' because they contain both comedy and tragedy.

2In 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', who does Egeus want his daughter, Hermia, to marry?
Hint
Consider that, in the world of the play, Egeus doesn't know that Oberon, King of the Fairies, exists.
Show answer
AnswerDemetrius
WhyThe play opens with Egeus furious with his daughter, Hermia, who wants to marry Lysander. This is not the man Egeus has picked for his daughter and he wants her to obey him, or be punished for her disobedience.
3In 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', why is Helena so unhappy at the beginning of the play?
Hint
Consider that Titania is the only woman who has the magical love potion put on her eyes.
Show answer
AnswerShe is in love with Demetrius, who doesn't love her back
WhyIn Act 1, Helena says of Demetrius, "The more I love, the more he hateth me." (1.1.199).
4In 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', why is Oberon so angry with Titania?
Hint
Consider that Titania is the victim of the magical love potion - it is used on her, she doesn't use it on anyone.
Show answer
Answerbecause she won't give him the little boy she is looking after
WhyIn Act 2, Oberon demands the boy from Titania: "Give me that boy" but Titania refuses, saying "Not for thy fairy kingdom." (2.1.143-4).
5In 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', what does Puck to do Bottom?
Hint
Consider that it is Titania who falls in love with Bottom because of Puck's use of the magical love potion.
Show answer
Answerturns his head into a donkey's head
WhyPuck sees Bottom practicing to perform in a play and thinks he is very silly. He transforms his head into that of a donkey partly as a joke on Bottom, but also so that Titania falls in love with someone completely ridiculous.
