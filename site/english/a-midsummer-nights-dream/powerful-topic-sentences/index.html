'A Midsummer Night's Dream': powerful topic sentences about Puck

View on Oak Academy

I can explain how Puck is presented in ‘A Midsummer Night’s Dream’ using evaluative and well supported topic sentences.

Content guidance
Depiction or discussion of discriminatory behaviourHistoric or outdated representations of people that may portray inequality or injustice. · Language and discrimination
Depiction or discussion of sexual violenceReferences to forced marriage, violence against women and girls. · Upsetting, disturbing and sensitive
Key learning points
  1. Topic sentences state a paragraph’s main idea.
  2. Evaluative topic sentences offer a judgement about a character.
  3. You could compare one character to another, or the complexity of a character, to create an evaluative topic sentence.
  4. Conjunctions can help create an evaluative topic sentence.
  5. You must support your evaluative topic sentence with appropriate evidence from the text.

Starter quiz

1In 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', what do we know about the character of Egeus?

Select all that apply.

Hint
Consider that Hermia chooses to run away from Athens.
Show answer
AnswerHe's unhappy with his daughter.; He threatens Hermia with severe punishments.
WhyThe play begins with Egeus telling Duke Theseus that he comes with "complaint" against his daughter Hermia. He is angry she wants to marry Lysander, rather than the man he has chosen for her. He threatens her with death if she doesn't obey him.
2Puck and Egeus are both characters in 'A Midsummer Night's Dream'. What are some of the differences between them?

Select all that apply.

Hint
Consider Puck's status in relation to Oberon. Where is he on the hierarchy?
Show answer
AnswerPuck is supernatural; Egeus is not.; Puck likes to play harmless pranks; Egeus threatens severe punishments.; Puck is a servant; Egeus is a powerful patriarch.
WhyComparing two characters can be a helpful way of understanding each deeper. Looking at the pain that Puck wants to inflict, compared to the punishment Egeus wants to inflict on his daughter perhaps puts Puck's pranks in perspective!
3In 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', both Oberon and Puck use the magical potion. What are the differences between how they use it?
Hint
Consider that Puck makes a mistake when he uses the magical potion on Lysander in Act 2, Scene 2.
Show answer
AnswerPuck accidently causes chaos; Oberon deliberately harms Titania.
WhyWhen Puck uses the magical potion on Lysander, he thinks he is Demetrius and is trying to make sure he falls in love with Helena. But when Oberon uses it on Titania, he says she hopes that she falls in love with something "vile".
4In 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', how do we know that Puck enjoys watching the Athenian nobles in chaos?

Select all that apply.

Hint
Consider that Puck leads the Athenians through the forest to restore order.
Show answer
AnswerHe says he finds watching unhappiness a fun hobby.; He asks Oberon if they can watch the chaos as entertainment.
WhyPuck calls the Athenian chaos a "fond pageant", meaning a funny show, and a "sport" meaning an enjoyable hobby. His reaction contrasts to Oberon's who is keen to restore order immediately.
5In 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', how do we know that Puck respects the hierarchy?

Select all that apply.

Hint
Consider that Puck transforms Bottom in Act 3, Scene 1. What has he already done by this point?
Show answer
AnswerHe obeys Oberon's orders swiftly and efficiently.; He obeys Oberon's orders even when he doesn't want to.; He only has fun with Bottom after he has fulfilled Oberon's orders.
WhyDespite being a mischief maker, Puck is presented as a very loyal servant both through his answers to Oberon's commands, and his actions which see him prioritise Oberon's wishes over his own.

Answertopic sentence
WhyA topic sentence gives a paragraph purpose and drive. It directs your thinking and reminds you what you should be focusing on throughout your paragraph.
2When writing about 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', you could start paragraphs with a topic sentence. You must support this topic sentence in the paragraph. What does it mean to support a topic sentence?
Hint
Consider that once you have stated your idea, you should develop rather than repeat it.
Show answer
AnswerTo select quotations from the text you are writing about that prove your idea.
WhyA supported topic sentence will require you to select evidence from the text. This can be one of the most enjoyable parts of planning as you get to return to the text itself and choose significant moments from it to analyse!
3In this topic sentence about 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', which word is the conjunction? "Shakespeare presents Puck as a mischief maker but his pranks are often harmless unlike Oberon's."
Hint
Consider that the word "presents" is the verb in this sentence.
Show answer
Answerbut
WhyA conjunction can help complicate an idea that you have by developing it. In this way, you can make your sentences evaluative.
4In this topic sentence about 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', which word is the conjunction? "Although Shakespeare presents Puck as an irreverent character, to Oberon he only ever shows respect."
Hint
Consider that Shakespeare is a proper noun, not a conjunction.
Show answer
AnswerAlthough
WhyA conjunction can help complicate an idea that you have. Here, the word "Although" pushes you to write about two sides of Puck's character.
5Which quotation would best support this topic sentence about 'A Midsummer Night's Dream': "Shakespeare presents Puck as a character who restores order despite his love of chaos."?
Hint
Consider which quotations best shows that Puck is key to restoring order.
Show answer
Answer"All shall be well." Act 3, Scene 2
WhyThis quotation ends Act 3, Scene 2 and sees an end to the chaos in the forest. Puck's words come at the end of a rhyming song-like sequence of dialogue that is reassuring after the chaos of the Athenian's fighting.
6Which quotation would best support this topic sentence about 'A Midsummer Night's Dream': "Shakespeare presents Puck as a character who is a devoted servant to Oberon despite his love of chaos."?
Hint
Consider which quotation shows Puck's responsiveness to Oberon's orders.
Show answer
Answer"I'll put a girdle round about the earth In forty minutes." Act 2, Scene 1
WhyThis quotation comes from the first dialogue between Oberon and Puck. Unlike his long explanation of all the tricks he likes to play on people to the fairy, here Puck is almost silent and merely responds to Oberon's orders quickly and efficiently.
