How is Puck presented in Shakespeare's 'A Midsummer Night's Dream'?

View on Oak Academy

I can select quotations which help me explore how Puck is presented across Acts 2 and 3 of 'A Midsummer Night's Dream'.

Key learning points
  1. Some people argue Puck is the most important character in the play because of his significance to the plot.
  2. Selecting powerful quotations is a key skill in English.
  3. Powerful quotations link to your topic sentence.
  4. Powerful quotations are rich in methods.
  5. You should select more than one quotation to prove each topic sentence.

2In 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', why does Puck help restore order at the end of Act 3?
Hint

Show answer
AnswerOberon orders him to.
WhyPuck makes it clear that he thinks the chaos in the forest is funny. He doesn't feel sorry for Titania, Bottom or any of the Athenian noble people. Significantly, he only restores order because Oberon orders him to.
3Complete this sentence stem: In 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', Puck wants to watch the Athenian nobles distress but...
Hint
Consider that Oberon applies the antidote to Titania's eyes.
Show answer
AnswerOberon commands him to restore order.
WhyUnlike Oberon who wants to restore order to the Athenian nobles immediately, Puck wants to watch them fighting. He considers watching unhappiness a fun hobby, and thinks of the Athenians themselves as "fools".

5Why might some people argue that Puck is the most important character in 'A Midsummer Night's Dream'?
