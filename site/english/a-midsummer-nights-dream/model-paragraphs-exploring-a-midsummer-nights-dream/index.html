Key learning points
  1. Annotating a question about ‘A Midsummer Night’s Dream’ ensures you focus on the right topic.
  2. Dramatic methods are the tools a playwright uses to create their work e.g. characterisation, setting, stage directions.
  3. When writing about the play, you should explore Shakespeare's dramatic methods.

1In Act 2, Scene 1 of 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', there is a fight between Oberon and Titania. Titania speaks many more lines than Oberon. What could we infer about the relationship between the two?

Select all that apply.

Hint
Consider that Oberon and Titania are of the same status: they are both monarchs.
Show answer
AnswerTitania is not scared of Oberon.; Titania is more dominant.
WhyLooking at Shakespeare's language is, of course, important. But it can also be useful to look at the balance of lines between characters, and see if it can reveal something about the power dynamic between them.
2In Act 2, Scene 1 of 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', after Titania leaves Oberon in the forest, what is the first thing he does?
Hint
Consider that Oberon doesn't know of Bottom's existence in Act 2.
Show answer
Answerstates he will get revenge on Titania because she continues to disobey him
WhyAs soon as Titania leaves, Oberon plans his revenge, perhaps suggesting the intensity of his anger and his ability for cruelty.
3When considering 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', you should explore Shakespeare's dramatic methods. Which of the following are examples of dramatic methods?

Select all that apply.

Hint
Consider that Shakespeare uses dramatic methods. He was a dramatist.
Show answer
Answerlanguage choices; stage directions
WhyAs pupils of English, our enjoyment of a play is two fold: first, we get to enjoy the story; second we get to enjoy exploring the dramatic methods which made the story so interesting and engaging. Language choices and stage directions are 2 examples.
4In Act 2, Scene 1 of 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', Oberon asks Titania, "Am I not thy...?".
Hint
Consider this is a title for a man of high status.
Show answer
Answerlord
WhyOberon's question seems to be a challenge to Titania. He is her lord, and therefore, he feels, she should obey him.
5In Act 2, Scene 1 of 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', Oberon asks Titania, "Am I not thy lord?". Titania replies, "Then I must be thy..."
Hint
Consider this is a title for a woman of high status.
Show answer
Answerlady / Lady
WhyTitania's reply shows her equality with Oberon. She matches his statement like for like. She then enlarges on her answer, and in this way dominates the dialogue during this scene.

Exit quiz

6 questions
1In 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', why might we describe the character of Oberon as malevolent?
Hint
Consider this word means cruel or malicious.
Show answer
Answerhe plots revenge on Titania
WhyShakespeare's Oberon is presented in many different ways. Sometimes he seems to want to help those around him, for example he wants to help Helena by making Demetrius fall in love with her. But his plan for Titania seems deliberately cruel.
2In Act 2, Scene 1 of 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', what does Titania want to do as soon as she sees Oberon?
Hint
Consider that Titania is presented as powerful in this scene, and doesn't need to run away from Oberon.
Show answer
Answerleave
WhyTitania says, "Fairies, skip hence." as her second sentence. She says she has sworn to stay away from Oberon because of his unreasonable demand that she give him the boy in her care.
3When given a question about 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', you should annotate it. What kinds of notes might you annotate it with?
Hint
Consider that knowing the plot is important, but you don't need to include it in most questions about the play unless you are being asked for a summary of the story.
Show answer
Answerthe focus of the question
WhyAnnotating an essay question is a short activity, but a crucial one. It makes sure that your thoughts are directed towards a certain aspect of the play, rather than everything you know about it. Annotating gives you a sense of purpose.
4In 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', Oberon, just like Egeus, is in conflict with an important person in his life. What are other similarities between the two men?

Select all that apply.

Hint
Consider that whilst Egeus speaks more lines than Hermia when they are arguing, Titania dominates the dialogue with Oberon.
Show answer
Answerthey both feel they are being disobeyed by women who should obey them; they both ask someone to support them punishing the person they are fighting
WhyComparing the differences and similarities between the conflicts in Acts 1 and 2 can help understand each in more detail, particularly the power dynamics between the characters.
5In Act 2, Scene 1 of 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', Oberon says, "Give me that boy" and Titania replies, "Not for thy ..."
Hint
Consider this is a two word phrase. It means the area over which Oberon rules.
Show answer
Answerfairy kingdom
WhyOberon's demand is met with a flat refusal, and the phrase "fairy kingdom" shows just how valuable the boy is to Titania - she won't give him up for anything.
6In Act 2, Scene 1 of 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', Titania says of Oberon, "I have his bed and company."
Hint
Consider this is an old fashioned word meaning to reject.
Show answer
Answerforesworn
WhyTitania's second line shows her strong resolve not to engage with Oberon because of his demand for the boy. She seems to hold the power at this point, saying it is she who has decided that they will not see each other.
