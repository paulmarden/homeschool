The significance of the magical forest in 'A Midsummer Night's Dream'

View on Oak Academy

I can explain why some of 'A Midsummer Night's Dream' takes place in a magical forest.

Key learning points
  1. Elizabethans believed that everyone had their place in society, a hierarchy they called The Great Chain of Being.
  2. To question or disrupt this hierarchy was a threat to society.
  3. The theatre was a place you could explore disorder because what was on stage wasn’t real life.
  4. It was even safer to explore disorder in a setting that wasn’t associated with law and order, a setting like a forest.
  5. Whilst much disorder takes place in ‘A Midsummer Night’s Dream’, order is restored at the end of the play.
Keywords

status quothe accepted standards and rules by which a community lives.
disorderconfusion, chaos.

1Shakespeare wrote 'A Midsummer Night's Dream' for an Elizabethan audience. As such, the play explores ideas that were important to Elizabethans. Match the key ideas and concepts to their meanings.
hierarchy
status quo
The Great Chain of Being
patriarchy
Hint
Start with patriarchy. Consider this word comes from the Greek patriarkhēs which means ruling father.
Show answer
Answerhierarchy → a system whereby people or things are ranked; status quo → the accepted standards and rules by which a community lives; The Great Chain of Being → a system of ranking which Elizabethans believed was created by God; patriarchy → a society in which men hold the power
WhyElizabethan society was highly structured but the theatre was a place to explore disorder in a way that was safe and didn't challenge, or suggest that people should challenge, the accepted rules of society.
2A pupil said of 'A Midsummer Night's Dream': "The forest is purely a place of disorder and chaos." How would you correct them?

Select all that apply.

Hint
Consider that Athens is presented as a place or order, rules and hierarchy.
Show answer
AnswerOberon and Puck make sure to restore the status quo.; The forest has a hierarchy (monarchs) so it is not complete chaos.
WhyIt is too simplistic to say that it is completely disordered in the forest. The hierarchy, Puck's obedience to Oberon, and Oberon and Puck's desire to restore the status quo means there is some evidence of structure and order.
3A pupil said of 'A Midsummer Night's Dream': "The forest is just as ordered as Athens." How would you correct them?

Select all that apply.

Hint
Consider that Oberon and Titania are King and Queen of the magical forest. What does that tell you about the forest's hierarchy?
Show answer
AnswerThe forest is a place where unexpected transformations happen, unlike in Athens.; The forest sees low and high status characters in relationships, unlike Athens.; There are no strict laws in the forest, unlike in Athens.
WhyAlthough there's some evidence of order in the forest (eg Puck's obedience to Oberon, or the hierarchy) there is a lot of chaos too. This disorder occurs and is resolved in the forest before the Athenians return to the ordered world of Athens.
4Elizabethans believed in a system of ranking called The Great Chain of Being. Starting with the most important, rank each section of society according Elizabethan ideas of importance.
Church
God
angels
animals
monarchs
noble people
people
Hint
Start at the top. Consider that Elizabethan England was a highly religious society. Most people were Christian and believed that God ruled every aspect of their lives.
Show answer
AnswerGod → angels → monarchs → Church → noble people → people → animals
WhyThis system of ranking impacted day to day life and attitudes. People believed that you were given your place in the hierarchy by God and thus to challenge your place, or those above you, was to challenge to God Himself.
5In 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', in what ways would the action in the forest disrupt Elizabethan ideas of the status quo?

Select all that apply.

Hint
Consider that the Athenian actors don't try and disrupt the status quo - they merely want to perform for the Duke. It is Puck who creates disorder in this group of characters.
Show answer
AnswerTwo characters are trying to marry without a parent's consent.; A high status character is in love with a low status character.
WhyElizabethan society was hierarchical and patriarchal. Exploring a world in which women disobey men (Hermia and Titania) or a Queen (Titania) falls in love with someone of lower status would only be acceptable on the stage, not in real life.
