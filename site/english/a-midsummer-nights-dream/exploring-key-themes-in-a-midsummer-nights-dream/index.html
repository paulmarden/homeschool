Key learning points
  1. A theme is an important idea in a text that is explored in a number of different ways.
  2. As students of English, the first step is to work out what are a text’s most important themes.
  3. The next step is to consider when these themes are explored in the text.
  4. Finally, you could consider what a writer might be trying to say about this particular theme.

AnswerEgeus → has power over his daughter's life; Theseus → has power over the city of Athens; Oberon → has power over Titania after he uses the magic potion; Puck → has power to transform himself and others
WhyPerhaps significantly, Shakespeare presents each of these characters as abusing their power, and using it for ill against those who are more vulnerable than they are.

Answerromantic relationships → explored through the Athenian nobles; friendships → explored through Hermia and Helena; mismatched relationships → explored through Titania and Bottom; father daughter relationships → explored through Hermia and Egeus; hierarchical relationships → explored through Puck and Oberon
