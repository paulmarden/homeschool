Introductions to essays about Puck in 'A Midsummer Night's Dream'

View on Oak Academy

I can write a well-structured introduction for an essay about the character of Puck in 'A Midsummer Night's Dream'.

Content guidance
Depiction or discussion of peer pressure or bullyingDepiction or discussion of distress or humiliation e.g. bullying. · Upsetting, disturbing and sensitive
Key learning points
  1. Introductions can follow a three part structure moving from the general to the specific.
  2. The first sentence of an introduction could be a general statement about the text.
  3. The second sentence of the introduction could be a general statement about the given character or theme.
  4. The final sentence of your introduction could be your thesis.
Keywords
capriciousimpulsive, reckless, changeable
thesisan idea you develop and maintain throughout an essay
general ideaan idea that is not tied to a particular detail
specific ideaan idea that is tied to a particular detail
supernaturalthings that can’t be explained by the laws of nature

You need access to a copy of William Shakespeare's 'A Midsummer Night's Dream'.

Hint

Show answer
AnswerAn introduction should start with a general statement about the text.
WhyAn introduction could follow a three part structure where the first sentence is a general statement about the text. In this way, you are preparing your reader to understand both the text, and your ideas about it.
3A pupil wrote: "'A Midsummer Night's Dream' is a play." as the first line of their introduction to an essay. What feedback might you give them?

WhyAn introduction should start with a general statement about the text. However, there is a fine balance between a general statement that helps a reader understand a text, and one which doesn't give any helpful insight into a text.

6In 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', why might we describe the character of Puck as capricious?

Select all that apply.

Hint
Consider this word means impulsive, reckless or changeable.
Show answer
AnswerHe decides to transform Bottom's head into that of a donkey.; He is known to play cruel and malicious tricks on people.
WhyShakespeare's language means that we are never quite sure of Puck - he transforms his attitude as well as how he looks depending on who he is talking to. His prank on Bottom is done on the spur of the moment, revealing an impulsive character.
