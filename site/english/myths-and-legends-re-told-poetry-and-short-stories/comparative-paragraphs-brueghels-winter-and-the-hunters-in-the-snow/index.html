Comparing poems: 'Brueghel's Winter' and 'The Hunters in the Snow'

View on Oak Academy

I can write a comparative paragraph which explores two poems inspired by the same painting.

Content guidance
Depiction or discussion of violence or sufferingDepiction or discussion of violence or fighting, including threats of violence or the aftermath of violence. · Upsetting, disturbing and sensitive
Depiction or discussion of violence or sufferingAnimal cruelty. · Upsetting, disturbing and sensitive
Key learning points
  1. The opening of a comparative paragraph could start with a topic sentence which states the paragraphs main idea.
  2. Next, the comparative paragraph could focus on analysing the first text.
  3. Then, a comparative paragraph could focus on the second text, comparing it to the first.
  4. Comparing the methods writers have used can help to develop the comparison.
  5. The final sentence of a comparative paragraph could link to the topic sentence.

Equipment
You need 'The Hunters in the Snow' by William Carlos Williams from the 1991 Collected Poems: 1939-1962, Volume II published by New Directions. You also need de la Mare's poem (additional materials).

Hint

Show answer
AnswerBoth de la Mare and Williams convey how treacherous the winter mountains can be. → De la Mare describes them as "Jagg'd" suggesting they are rough and pointed. → By contrast, Williams describes them as "icy" suggesting they are slippery. → Ultimately, both poets convey that the mountains have the capacity to hurt us.
WhyA comparative paragraph requires us to analyse 2 texts, which can sometimes feel challenging. A helpful structure can be to start with a topic sentence followed by analysis/comparison of quotations and a concluding sentence summing up your ideas.
