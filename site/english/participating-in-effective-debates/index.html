Unit 3 of 9

Participating in effective debates

View on Oak Academy

In this unit, pupils learn about debate and practise having debates. They first learn what a debate is, before presenting arguments for a debate and then learning how you create rebuttals in debates. Pupils then finish the unit by having a debate.

4 lessons

01What is a formal debate?
I can understand how a formal debate is structured and consider my role within it.02Creating and presenting opening arguments for a debate
I can prepare an effective opening argument for both sides of a debate and present them successfully.03Responding in debates: effective rebuttals
I can identify key points or flaws in an opposing argument and use these to create an effective rebuttal.04Time to debate: planning, evaluating and participating
I can effectively plan and confidently participate in a debate, and use a success criteria to evaluate my contribution.
