Analytical paragraphs: Maya Angelou’s poetic voice

View on Oak Academy

I can explore Maya Angelou's poetic voice in well-structured analytical paragraphs.

Key learning points
  1. We can make comments about Angelou's poetic voice by considering a number of her poems at the same time.
  2. When considering Angelou's poetic voice in an analytical paragraph, you should include (and explore) quotations.
  3. When considering Angelou's poetic voice in an analytical paragraph, you could include context to develop your ideas.
  4. A single paragraph outline is a way of planning a paragraph.
  5. First and final sentences of a single paragraph outline are written in full; supporting detail is written in note form.

Equipment
You need access to 'Phenomenal Woman', 'Woman Work', 'Still I Rise', 'On Aging', 'Caged Bird', 'Equality', 'Life Doesn't Frighten Me', and 'On the Pulse of the Morning' (Penguin Random House).

Hint

Answersupporting detail
WhyWriting your topic and concluding sentences in full means you know the main focus on your paragraph. Making notes about the supporting detail mean that you can develop these ideas further when you come to write the paragraph in full.
3Match each of these terms to their definitions ('Single poet study: Maya Angelou').
topic sentence
supporting detail
concluding sentence
analysis
context
Hint
Consider that the information that Maya Angelou experienced life during the Jim Crow laws is an example of context for the poem 'Caged Bird'.
Show answer
Answertopic sentence → first sentence of a paragraph stating its main focus; supporting detail → ideas which prove your topic sentence; concluding sentence → sums up main ideas in your paragraph; analysis → comments on why a writer has written something in a certain way; context → information about how, why and when the text was written
WhyHaving a good understanding of each of these terms will help you understand what it means to write an analytical paragraph. For example, analysis means making comments on how a text was written.
