The structure of an opinion article

View on Oak Academy

I can explain how to create engaging openings and impactful endings in an opinion piece.

Content guidance
Depiction or discussion of upsetting contentDepiction of scary, confusing or unsettling events or characters placed in danger, including creation of a scary atmosphere through the use of music and sound. · Upsetting, disturbing and sensitive
Depiction or discussion of sensitive contentSubjects which particular individuals may be sensitive to, eg, adoption, migration, bereavement or death, divorce, poverty, deprivation. · Upsetting, disturbing and sensitive
Key learning points
  1. You can create an engaging opening by using humour, or creating a shared experience with your reader.
  2. In an opinion piece, your message is your opinion.
  3. In an opinion piece, it is important to get the reader onside, so that they are more likely to internalise your message.
  4. The ending of your opinion piece should bring together the threads of your argument and reinforce your viewpoint.
  5. You can create an impactful ending by using mirroring and/or leaving your reader with a moral conundrum to consider.
Keywords
impactfulmemorable and significant.
to implyto indicate the truth about something without explicitly saying it.
viewpointa person’s opinion or perspective on something.
to reinforceto strengthen or further support something - in this context - an idea.
conundruma confusing and difficult-to-answer problem or question.
