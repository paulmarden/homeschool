Using semantic fields to create a gothic atmosphere

View on Oak Academy

I can use semantic fields to create a restrictive atmosphere.

Content guidance
Depiction or discussion of violence or sufferingDepiction or discussion of violence or fighting, including threats of violence or the aftermath of violence. · Upsetting, disturbing and sensitive
Key learning points
  1. A semantic field is a set of words that are related in meaning.
  2. Semantic fields can challenge us to use more ambitious vocabulary.
  3. Semantic fields can help us to be less repetitive when selecting our vocabulary.
  4. It can be useful to plan your use of vocabulary (within the semantic field) before you begin writing.
Keywords
semantic fielda group of words that all share similar meanings
connotationsthe associations we make with a word - what the word reminds us of
vividproducing strong, clear images in the mind
oppressiveif something is oppressive, it makes people feel uncomfortable or it is cruel and unfair
