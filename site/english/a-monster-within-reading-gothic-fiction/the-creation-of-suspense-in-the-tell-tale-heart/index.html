The creation of suspense in 'The Tell-Tale Heart'

View on Oak Academy

I can identify the methods used by Poe to create suspense.

Content guidance
Depiction or discussion of serious crimeDepictions of murder. · Upsetting, disturbing and sensitive
Depiction or discussion of mental health issuesDepiction or discussion of serious mental health issues including eating disorders, self-harm, suicide or attempted suicide, or the aftermath of such an event. · Upsetting, disturbing and sensitive
Key learning points
  1. The narrator in the story is extremely hubristic, unreliable and unpredictable because he is delusional.
  2. Poe uses the hubristic and delusional narrator to create suspense.
  3. Poe uses irony to create suspense - the narrator insists that he is not ‘mad’ but his actions suggest otherwise.
  4. Poe shortens his sentences and uses more expressive punctuation to reflect the narrator’s escalating delusion.
  5. Poe uses sentence structure and punctuation to build tension in the lead up to the narrator's dramatic confession.
Keywords
suspensea state of feeling anxious or excited about what could happen
hubristicexcessively proud or arrogant
unreliablenot to be relied or depended on
heinousextremely wicked
ironythe expression of meaning by using language that suggests the opposite of the reality
