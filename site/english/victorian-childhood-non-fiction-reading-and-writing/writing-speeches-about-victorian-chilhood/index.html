Writing speeches about supporting Victorian working class children

View on Oak Academy

I can write a well-structure persuasive speech about supporting Victorian working class children.

Content guidance
Depiction or discussion of discriminatory behaviourHistoric or outdated representations of people that may portray inequality or injustice. · Language and discrimination
Key learning points
  1. Open a speech in a dynamic way to engage your audience.
  2. Rhetorical devices should be used to support arguments in persuasive speeches.
  3. If you are trying to persuade a listener, include a call to action so they can make a tangible change.
  4. Punctuate speeches accurately to support with delivery.
Keywords
Destituedestitute means extremely poor and unable to look after oneself.
Impoverisheda person or area that has been made poor can be described as being impoverished.
Desperatedesperate means a feeling that a situation is so bad it is impossible to deal with.
Civilised societya civilised society is one that treats people who live there fairly, with a fair justice system.
Imploreto implore means to beg someone to do something.

Hint
Which question is the most simple to give a clear answer to?
Show answer
AnswerWhat is the capital city of England?
WhyA rhetorical question does not require and answer and instead directs a reader to think about a specific topic the speaker is guiding them to.

Hint
Look for the statement that could not be directed to an audience.
Show answer
AnswerI feel ashamed that I have not done more.
WhyBoth 'you' and 'the great people in this room' are referring to an audience/listener.
3Which of the following does **not** use the word 'desperate' correctly?
Hint
Desperate is an adjective.
Show answer
AnswerThe child needed food desperate.
WhyIn this sentence the adverb desperately should be used.
4What is a topic sentence?
Hint
Focus and topic have a similar meaning.
Show answer
AnswerA sentence which explains the upcoming focus of a paragraph.
WhyTopic sentences signpost to the reader what the focus/topic will be in the upcoming paragraph.

Hint
The word means something that is done.
Show answer
Answeraction / Action
WhyA call to action is when you urge the listener to take action and make a change.

Exit quiz

6 questions
1What is the most effective way to structure a speech? Place the sections in the correct order.
A cohesive conclusion which includes a call to action.
A hook which engages your audience.
Clear arguments supported by rhetorical devices.
Hint
Always start with a hook.
Show answer
AnswerA hook which engages your audience. → Clear arguments supported by rhetorical devices. → A cohesive conclusion which includes a call to action.
WhyStart with a hook, before introducing each of your arguments. Finally end with a call to action.
2Which **two** words mean poor.

Select all that apply.

Hint
Can you spot the word that is derived from poverty?
Show answer
AnswerImpoverished; Destitute
WhyBoth impoverished and destitute are synonyms for poor.
3Why is using "Dear Members of Parliament" inappropriate for a speech?
Hint
What type of writing would you most usually start with 'dear'?
Show answer
AnswerIt sounds like you are writing to them, rather than delivering a speech.
WhyWhen writing a speech, you should always consider words which indicate your speech is going to be spoken out loud. 'Dear' could imply you are writing to parliament, rather than delivering a speech.
4Which of the following is the most emotive sentence?
Hint
Look for emotive adjectives and verbs.
Show answer
AnswerInnocent children perish on the streets.
WhyThe adjective innocent is more emotive than young. The verb 'to perish' suggests being left to die, which is more emotive than 'left.'
5Which of the following is the most emotive sentence?
Hint
Look for the most emotive adjectives and verbs.
Show answer
AnswerImpoverished children are being denied access to education.
WhyBeing denied access to education implies people are actively stopping children accessing education, which feels more emotive.
6Which of the following is **not** a justifiable reason for using accurate punctuation when writing a speech?
Hint
Why do we use paragraphs?
