Developing active listening skills

View on Oak Academy

I can use effective active listening skills to be a present and responsive audience member.

Content guidance
Depiction or discussion of discriminatory behaviourHistoric or outdated representations of people that may portray inequality or injustice. · Language and discrimination
Key learning points
  1. Listening is an important skill - it helps to create a supportive environment and can make you better at responding.
  2. Active listening means you are completely engaged and focused on what is being delivered.
  3. Using positive body language can help demonstrate active listening and keep you focused on the content.
  4. Part of being an active listener, is being able to respond effectively.
  5. Open questions can develop comments and encourage discussion.
Keywords
active listeningfully concentrating, understanding and responding thoughtfully to what someone is saying
body languagenon-verbal cues or gestures such as facial expressions and posture
engagedactively participating and focused, to be attentive and show interest and enthusiasm
open questiona type of question that allows for a wide range of responses beyond just 'yes' or 'no'
