Unit 6 of 9

Victorian childhood: non-fiction reading and writing

View on Oak Academy

In this unit, pupils read a mixture of contemporary and nineteenth-century non-fiction texts that explore a common theme of childhood. They first read and compare the texts, before then writing and delivering a speech as part of a debate on Victorian working class children.
