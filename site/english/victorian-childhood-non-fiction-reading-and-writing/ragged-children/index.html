Education for poor Victorian children

View on Oak Academy

I can explain how emotive language is used to discuss poverty in Victorian Britain.

Content guidance
Depiction or discussion of discriminatory behaviourHistoric or outdated representations of people that may portray inequality or injustice. · Language and discrimination
Key learning points
  1. There was no compulsory schooling in Victorian Britain.
  2. Education was a luxury only the rich could afford.
  3. Ragged schools were designed to provide education to the poorest in society.
  4. When analysing a writer's language choices, topic sentences should focus on effect rather than subject terminology.
  5. Topic sentences which focus on effect give opportunity to make connections between quotations from across a text.
Keywords
Correlationif two things have a mutual relationship or connection, they are are said to have a correlation.
Illiteracyilliteracy means being unable, or struggling, to read and write.
Destitutedestitute means extremely poor and unable to look after oneself.
Pleaa plea is a request made in an urgent or emotional manner.
Ragged schoolsragged schools were schools which provided education to children who were too poor to receive it elsewhere.

Hint
Plea is a noun.

WhyThe other two examples use plea as a verb. A plea is a noun.
5There are three determiners in the following sentence. Can you name one? 'The boy ate some dinner. He only had a small plate of food.'
Hint
A determiner is a modifying word that determines how much there is of a specific noun.
Show answer
AnswerThe / some / a / the
WhyAll of these words show modify a noun, telling us how many or how much there is of something e.g. there is one boy but he eats more than one piece of food.
6At the start of Queen Victoria's reign, who had access to education?
Hint
Do you think there is a relationship between access to education and poverty?
Show answer
AnswerOnly people who could afford to pay for education.
WhyEducation was not free at the start of Victoria's reign. The cost of schooling meant that some parents could not afford to send their children to school. There was no law which said that parents had to send their children to school either.

WhyWhere possible, we should always try and use more than one quotation to justify our topic sentences.
