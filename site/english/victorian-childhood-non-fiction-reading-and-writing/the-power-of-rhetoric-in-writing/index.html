Writing a persuasive speech about supporting Victorian children

View on Oak Academy

I can identify how language and structural devices have been used to make a speech powerful.

Content guidance
Depiction or discussion of discriminatory behaviourHistoric or outdated representations of people that may portray inequality or injustice. · Language and discrimination
Key learning points
  1. Emotive language requires you to select the sensitivity of your words to elicit specific emotions from the audience.
  2. Excellent rhetorical questions use emotive language to enhance the complexity of the question.
  3. Great rhetorical questions should use direct address subtly, avoiding overuse of ‘you’.
  4. Anaphora is a useful tool is structuring persuasive writing.

5Which of the following rhetorical questions uses direct address in the least subtle way?
Hint

Show answer
AnswerWould you let this to happen to your child?
WhyWhilst this sentence is fine for us to use in our writing, always referring to the audience as 'you' is less subtle. Sometimes this can be less effective in persuading an audience as they can feel attacked.
6Which of the following avoids using pronouns when using direct address, to create a **subtle** rhetorical question?
Hint
Subtle means in a clever and unobvious way.

AnswerShould our great nation find this acceptable?
WhyBy referring to 'our great nation' this is a subtle reminder that we are part of a community and therefore responsible. It makes the audience feel part of a community, rather than singled out by the speaker.
