Properties of prisms

View on Oak Academy

I can appreciate that all prisms have two congruent polygonal parallel faces (bases) with parallelogram faces joining the corresponding vertices of the bases.

Key learning points
  1. Prisms are 3D shapes with a cross-section that is a uniform polygon.
  2. All prisms will have a pair of parallel faces that are congruent.
  3. This polygon cross section is often used to name the prism.
  4. The sides connecting the bases are parallelograms.
Keywords
Prism
A prism is a polyhedron with a base that is a polygon and a parallel opposite face that is identical.
Cross-section
A cross section is a 2D face made from cutting straight through any plane of a 3D object.
