Perimeter of composite shapes

View on Oak Academy

I can solve perimeter problems of composite shapes involving whole and/or part circles.

Key learning points
  1. The length of fractions of the circumference of a circle can be found.
  2. The perimeter of parts of a circle can be found using the formula for the circumference and reasoning.
  3. The perimeter of shapes made from circles and parts of circles can be found using the circumference and reasoning.
  4. The perimeter of composite shapes including circles can be found.
Keywords
Arc
An arc is part of a curve. When describing an arc of a circle, it is part of the circle's circumference.
