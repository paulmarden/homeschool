Checking understanding of perimeter

View on Oak Academy

I can find the perimeter of many polygons or compound shapes.

Key learning points
  1. The perimeter of any polygon can be found by summing the sides.
  2. The perimeter of shapes made from polygons can be found.
  3. Some methods for finding perimeters are more efficient than others.
  4. Missing lengths can be found when the perimeter is known.
Keywords
perimeter
the distance around a 2D shape
polygon
flat (2D), closed figure made up of straight line segments
compound/composite shape
a shape created using two or more basic shapes
