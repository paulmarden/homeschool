Surface area of cylinders

View on Oak Academy

I can find the surface area of cylinders.

Key learning points
  1. Drawing the net of a cylinder helps deduce the formula for the surface area.
  2. The formula can be used to find the surface area of any cylinder.
  3. Unknown lengths can be found when the surface area is known.
Keywords
Cylinder
a 3D shape with a base that is a circle and a parallel opposite face that is identical. Cross sections parallel to the base are uniform.

Answer64 / sixty four
WhyThe radius is 8 cm, so the area is $$8^2 \times \pi$$. $$64\pi$$ is approximately 201 $$\text{ cm}^2$$.
4The surface area is the total area of all the surfaces of a closed shape. The surfaces include all faces and any curved surfaces.
Hint
Cubes, spheres, triangular prisms and cones are all examples of these type of shapes.

5A cuboid has dimensions 4 cm, 6 cm and 15 cm. Select all the areas of its surfaces.

Select all that apply.

Hint
A cuboid has rectangular faces. One of the rectangles will has an area of 6 cm × 4 cm
Show answer
Answer$$$24\text{ cm}^2$$; $$$60\text{ cm}^2$$; $$$90\text{ cm}^2$$
WhyFind the area of each rectangular face. 6 cm × 4 cm = 24 cm²; 4 cm × 15 cm = 60 cm²; 6 cm × 15 cm = 90 cm².
6A cube has a surface area of 294 $$\text{ cm}^2$$. The area of each face is $$\text{ cm}^2$$.
Hint
A cube has six identical faces. The surface area is therefore 6 lots of the area of each face.
Show answer
Answer49
WhyThere are 6 faces. So the area of one face is 294 cm² ÷ 6 = 49 cm².

4Which of these is the formula for the surface area of any cylinder, where $$r$$ is the radius and $$h$$ is the height?

Select all that apply.

Hint
The surface area of a cylinder is the sum of the two circle's areas and the curved surface which is a rectangle with dimensions of the circumference of the circle and the height of the cylinder.
Show answer
Answer$$$\text{Surface area}=2\pi r^2+2\pi rh$$; $$$\text{Surface area}=2\pi r(r+h)$$
WhyThe surface area is $$\text{Surface area}=2\pi r^2+2\pi rh$$ as the curved surface unfolds to be a rectangle with dimensions: circumference of the circle by the height of the cylinder. You can factorise this to find another formula.
5The surface area of a cylinder with a diameter of 10 cm and a height of 7 cm is $$\pi \text{ cm}^2.$$
Hint
You have been told the diameter of the cylinder, whereas the radius is needed for the area of a circle.
Show answer
Answer120
WhyRadius of circular end face = 5 cm. So the surface area = $$2\pi \times 5^2+2\pi \times 5 \times 7$$
6A cylinder has a surface area of $$72\pi \text{ cm}^2$$ and a radius of 3 cm. What is the length of the cylinder?
Hint
Subtract the area of the two circular faces from the surface area first. This will then give you the area of the curved face which has the dimensions: circumference of the circle by the height of the cylinder.
Show answer
Answer9 cm
WhyArea of circular end faces = $$2 \times \pi \times 3^2=18$$ cm². So, area of curved face = $$72 \pi - 18 \pi = 54 \pi$$ cm². Area of curved face = $$2 \times \pi \times 3 \times h = 54 \pi$$. So, $$h = 9$$
