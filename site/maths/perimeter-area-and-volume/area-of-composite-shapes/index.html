Area of composite shapes

View on Oak Academy

I can solve area problems of composite shapes involving whole and/or part circles.

Key learning points
  1. The area parts of a circle can be found using the area formula and reasoning.
  2. The area of shapes made from circles and parts of circles can be found.
  3. The area of composite shapes that include circles can be found.
Keywords
sector
A sector is the region formed between two radii and their connecting arc.
