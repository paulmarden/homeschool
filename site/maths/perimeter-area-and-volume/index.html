Unit 8 of 10

Perimeter, area and volume

View on Oak Academy

In this unit pupils extend their knowledge of shapes by exploring the properties of circles and composite shapes before developing understanding of 3-D shapes including surface area and volume.

16 lessons

01Checking understanding of perimeter
I can find the perimeter of many polygons or compound shapes.02Checking understanding of area
I can find the area of triangles, quadrilaterals and composite rectilinear shapes.03Multiplicative relationships in circles
I can recognise that there is a constant multiplicative relationship (π) between the diameter and circumference of a circle.04Circumference of a circle
I can use the relationship C=πd to calculate unknown lengths in contexts involving the circumference of circles.05Area of a circle
I can understand the derivation of the area of a circle.06Using the formula for the area of a circle
I can use the formula for the area of a circle to calculate either the area or a missing measurement.07Area of composite shapes
I can solve area problems of composite shapes involving whole and/or part circles.08Perimeter of composite shapes
I can solve perimeter problems of composite shapes involving whole and/or part circles.09Finding a length in composite shapes
I can solve area problems of composite shapes involving whole and/or part circles where the area is known and the radius or diameter must be found.10Surface area of cuboids
I can appreciate the concept of surface area and find the surface area of cuboids.11Properties of prisms
I can appreciate that all prisms have two congruent polygonal parallel faces (bases) with parallelogram faces joining the corresponding vertices of the bases.12Surface area of prisms
I can find the surface area of 3D shapes in an efficient way.13Surface area of cylinders
I can find the surface area of cylinders.14Volume of prisms
I can use the constant cross-sectional area property of prisms to determine their volume.15Volume of cylinders
I can use the constant cross-sectional area property of cylinders to determine their volume.16Problem solving with perimeter, area and volume
I can use my knowledge of perimeter, area and volume to solve problems.
