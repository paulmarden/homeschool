Problem solving with estimation and rounding

View on Oak Academy

I can use my knowledge of estimation and rounding to solve problems.

Key learning points
  1. It may be helpful to estimate an answer to check the magnitude is correct.
  2. It can be useful to use rounding to significant figures when dealing with very big or very small numbers in context.
  3. Estimates can help when making decisions about maximum or minimum possible answers in real life contexts.
  4. You can use Fermi estimation to find approximate solutions to problems.
Keywords
Estimate
A quick estimate for a calculation is obtained from using approximate values, often rounded to 1 significant figure.

1A degree of accuracy shows how precise a or measurement is. For example, to the nearest cm, nearest 10, 1 s.f. and so on.
Hint
Gram, kilogram, millimetre, centimetre, litre are all units of measurement. What other things do you round?
Show answer
Answernumber / value
WhyA degree of accuracy shows how precise a number or measurement is. For example you may round the number of people in a cinema to the nearest 10.
2Alex buys paper £4.95, USB £$$\square$$, stamps £5.20, crisps £0.95, staples £1.10 and plug £1.99. He pays with a £20 note and receives about £2 back in change. Estimate the cost of the USB.
Hint
Round the numbers to the appropriate degree of accuracy and subtract from £18 (This is how much all the items cost). This will give you the price of the USB.
Show answer
Answer£4
WhyAn example rounding for this question is 18 − 5 − 5 − 1 − 1 − 2 = 4, so the price of the USB should be approximately £4.
3An error interval for a number $$y$$ shows the range of possible values of $$y$$. It is written as:
Hint
You want to include the number $$a$$, but not include the number $$b$$.
Show answer
Answer$$$ a\leq y < b$$
Why$$$ a\leq y < b$$ means you are including the values of $$a$$ as the smallest and not including the value of $$b$$ as the largest.
4When a number, $$x$$, is rounded to the nearest integer the result is 88. Identify the error interval.
Hint
Drawing a number line with 88 in the centre and, given the degree of accuracy is an integer, put 89 and 87 either side of the 88. Identify the middle of 87 and 88 and the middle of 88 and 89.
Show answer
Answer$$$87.5\leq x< 88.5$$
WhyThe smallest number when rounded to the nearest integer is 87.5, but the largest cannot be 88.5 as this rounds to 89. You show a number which is infinitely smaller than 88.5 but not equal to 88.5 using this error interval $$87.5\leq x<88.5$$
5A triangle has lengths 3.5 cm (2 s.f), 4.1 cm (2 s.f) and 4.8 cm (2 s.f). Write the error interval for the perimeter $$p$$ of the triangle.
Hint
Identify the error interval of all three lengths then sum the lowest limits and the upper limits to make the error interval.
Show answer
Answer$$$ 12.25 \leq p < 12.55 $$
WhyThe error intervals of the three sides are $$ 3.45\leq x < 3.55 $$, $$ 4.05 \leq x < 4.15 $$ and $$ 4.75\leq x < 4.85 $$. Therefore summing the lower and upper limits the answer is $$ 12.25 \leq p < 12.55 $$
6A square has lengths in centimetres to 1 decimal place. The error interval of the area of the square is $$17.2225 \leq x <18.0625 $$. Work out the length of the square to 1 d.p.
Hint
Square root the limits to the error interval. This will give you the upper and lower limits of the length.
Show answer
Answer4.2 cm
Why$$$\sqrt{17.2225} \leq x <\sqrt{18.0625} $$ gives $$4.15\leq x <4.25$$. Therefore side length is the number in between giving an answer of 4.2 cm

5Fermi problem : How long will it take to count to 1 million (without breaks, sleep, etc)?
Hint
Assuming you count 1 second for each number, how many seconds would it take to count to 1 000 000? Then convert to minutes, hours and then days.
Show answer
Answer16 days
Why$$$1\;000\;000\div60\approx 1\;000\;000\div 50=20\;000$$ minutes, $$20\;000\div60\approx 20\;000\div 50=400 $$ hours, $$400\div24\approx 400\div 25=16 $$ days
6Fermi problem : If you put 1 million pound coins in a box, what is the volume of the box in m³ ?
Hint
A £1 coin has a diameter of 22 mm and a thickness of 3 mm.
Show answer
Answer1.2 m³
WhyDiameter = 22 mm and a thickness = 3 mm. Treat each coin as a cuboid. So volume is $$22\times22\times3\approx 20\times20\times3=1200$$ mm$$^3$$. Therefore $$1200\times 1\;000\;000 = 1\;200\;000\;000$$ mm³ = 1.2 m³
