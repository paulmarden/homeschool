Checking understanding of rounding

View on Oak Academy

I can round to the nearest 100 or 10 or whole number or to 1 or 2 decimal places.

Key learning points
  1. Any number can be rounded to the nearest 10, 100 or 1000.
  2. A number with one or more decimal places can be rounded to the nearest whole number.
  3. A number with two or more decimal places can be rounded to one decimal place.
  4. A number with three or more decimal places can be rounded to two decimal places.
Keywords
Round
To change a number into another number that is approximately the same value but is easier to work with. E.g. round 561 to the nearest 10 is 560.
