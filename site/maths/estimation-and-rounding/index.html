Unit 1 of 10

Estimation and rounding

View on Oak Academy

This unit helps pupils understand rounding to different decimal places, and significant figures. Pupils will also learn how to truncate numbers and understand the contexts in which these skills are useful. The unit ends will an exploration of rounding and inequality errors.

Number

Why this, why now

Students up to this point have been working with integer values and have had few opportunities to work explicitly on estimation. We are starting the year with something that is familiar for students and extend knowledge of estimation and rounding to include more work on rounding with decimals and significant figures.

Prior knowledge
Round numbers to nearest 10, 100, 1000
Round decimals to 1 or 2 decimal places
Estimate calculations using compatible numbers

I can round to the nearest 100 or 10 or whole number or to 1 or 2 decimal places.02Securing understanding of rounding
I can recognise when values have been rounded and state sensible suggestions of what the unrounded value could have been.03Rounding to three decimal places
I can round numbers to three decimal places.04Rounding to any number of decimal places
I can round numbers to any number of decimal places.05Rounding integers to one significant figure
I can round integers to one significant figure.06Rounding integers to significant figures
I can round integers to a required number of significant figures.07Rounding decimals to significant figures
I can round decimals to a required number of significant figures.08Degrees of accuracy
I can appreciate what is meant by a suitable degree of accuracy.09Estimating numerical calculations
I can estimate numerical calculations.10Checking by estimating
I can estimate and check if solutions to problems are of the correct magnitude.11Truncating
