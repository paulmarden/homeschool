Rounding decimals to significant figures

View on Oak Academy

I can round decimals to a required number of significant figures.

Key learning points
  1. Numbers bigger than 1 with decimals places can be rounded to significant figures.
  2. When working with numbers smaller than one it is important to carefully identify the first significant figure.
  3. Once the first significant figure is identified you can find any significant figure.
  4. It is important to know when zeros are and are not significant.
Keywords
Significant figure
Significant figures are the digits in a number that contribute to the accuracy of the number. The first significant figure is the first non-zero digit.
Round
To change a number into another number that is approximately the same value but is easier to work with. E.g. round 561 to the nearest 10 is 560.
