Checking by estimating

View on Oak Academy

I can estimate and check if solutions to problems are of the correct magnitude.

Key learning points
  1. Estimation should give you an idea of the size of an answer.
  2. It is useful to estimate the answer to calculations so you can check your answers make sense.
  3. Errors in calculations can be found by using estimates.
Keywords
Significant figures
Significant figures are the digits in a number that contribute to the accuracy of the number.
Estimate
A quick estimate for a calculation is obtained from using approximate values, often rounded to 1 significant figure.

Answer1000 × 200; 1000 × 210; 1000 × 213
WhyAll except $9 × 2 are good estimates. As the 989 has been rounded to 1000, this number can be multiplied easily.
3Which of the following are good, quick estimate calculations for 432.4 ÷ 1.85?

Select all that apply.

Hint
Rounding to a certain degree of accuracy ensure your estimate calculation is appropriate. Also, remember the calculation must allow you to make a "quick" estimate too.
Show answer
Answer400 ÷ 2; 500 ÷ 2
Why400 ÷ 2 is a good estimate, as each number has been rounded to 1 significant figure. 500 ÷ 2 is good estimate too, as 432 has been rounded to the nearest 500 without losing too much accuracy. 400 ÷ 1.85 is not a quick estimate calculation.

Significant figures are the digits in a number that contribute to the accuracy of the number. When rounding to decimal places, you include the number of decimal places stated in the degree of accuracy.

Answer9212 → 9000 (1 s.f.); 8934.89 → 8934.9 (1 d.p.); 8910 → 8900 (2 s.f.); 8934.894 → 8934.89 (2 d.p.)
Why8934.894 = 9000 (1 s.f.) and 8934.894 = 8900 (2 s.f.). You identify the place value column to the right of the relevant significant figure and round up or down. 8934.894=8934.9 (1 d.p.) as you only include 1 digit to the right of the decimal place.
