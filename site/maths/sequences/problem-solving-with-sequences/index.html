Problem solving with sequences

View on Oak Academy

I can use my knowledge of sequences to solve problems.

Key learning points
  1. Patterns can be described using sequences.
  2. Sequences can be found for given terms even if they are not sequential .
  3. It is possible to identify which term in a sequence a number is from the rule.
  4. Real life sequences can be represented on a graph.
Keywords
Arithmetic sequence
An arithmetic (or linear) sequence is a sequence where the difference between successive terms is a constant.
nth term
The nth term of a sequence is the position of a term in a sequence where n stands for the term number.

4What is the $$n^\text{th}$$ term of the arithmetic sequence 8,9,10,11, ...?
Hint
To find the $$n^\text{th}$$ term of an arithmetic sequence you need to know the common difference, and the translation.
Show answer
Answer$$$n+7$$
WhyCommon difference of +1 making it a $$1n$$ sequence. 8,9,10,11, ... is a translation of +7 from the sequence 1,2,3,4, ... so this sequence is $$1n+7$$ which we write more simply as $$n+7$$.
5What is the $$n^\text{th}$$ term of the arithmetic sequence 8,11,14,17 ...?
Hint
To find the $$n^\text{th}$$ term of an arithmetic sequence you need to know the common difference, and the translation.
Show answer
Answer$$$3n+5$$
WhyCommon difference of +3 making it a $$3n$$ sequence. 8,11,14,17 ... is a translation of +5 from the sequence 3,6,9,12, ... so this sequence is $$3n+5$$.
6The 118$$^\text{th}$$ term of the sequence $$5050-7n$$ is 4224. What is the 113$$^\text{th}$$ term?
Hint
The coefficient of $$n$$ tells us the common difference in the $$n^\text{th}$$ term of an arithmetic sequence.
Show answer
Answer4259
WhyCounting backwards in the sequence: $$n=118, T=4224$$, $$n=117, T=4231$$, $$n=116, T=4238$$, $$n=115, T=4245$$, $$n=114, T=4252$$,$$n=113, T=4259$$. The $$113^{th}$$ term is $$4259$$.

2The 2$$^\text{nd}$$ term of an arithmetic sequence is 11 and the 6$$^\text{th}$$ term is 47. What is the 1$$^\text{st}$$ term of the sequence?
Hint
What is the difference between 11 and 47 and over how many steps was it taken?
Show answer
Answer2
Why47 - 11 = 36 From $$n=2$$ to $$n=6$$ there are 4 steps. $$36 \div 4=9$$ so we have a common difference of +9. If the 2$$^\text{nd}$$ term is 11 the first term must be 2
3Buses leave Wallsend for Byker at regular intervals. The second bus of the day leaves at 6:44 am. You just missed the seventh bus of the day which left at 8:04 am. What time is the next bus?
Hint
How many minutes from 6:44 am to 8:04 am and how many buses in that time period?
Show answer
Answer8:20 am
WhyFrom 6:44 am to 8:04 am there are 80 minutes. $$80 \div 5=16$$ minute intervals. Adding 16 minutes to 8:04 am gives is a time of 8:20 am.
4The 24$$^\text{th}$$ term of an arithmetic sequence is 281. The 37$$^\text{th}$$ term is 437. Which calculation will be the starting point to finding the $$n^\text{th}$$ term of the sequence?
Hint
How do we find the difference between 281 and 437? How do we find how many steps this was taken over?
Show answer
Answer$$${437 - 281}\over{37-24} $$
Why$$${437 - 281}\over{37-24} $$ tells us that it is actually $$12n-7$$.

Hint

WhyThe numerators 2, 9, 16, 23, ... form an arithmetic sequence of $$n^\text{th}$$ term $$7n-5$$. The denominators 3,8,13,18, ... are the sequence $$5n-2$$ so this fractional sequence is $${7n-5} \over {5n-2}$$
