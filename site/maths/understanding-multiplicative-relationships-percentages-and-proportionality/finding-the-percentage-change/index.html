Finding the percentage change

View on Oak Academy

I can find the percentage increase or decrease given start and finish quantities.

Key learning points
  1. The percentage change can be found using a bar model.
  2. The percentage change can be found using a double number line.
  3. The percentage change can be found using a ratio table.
Keywords
Proportion
Variables are in proportion if they have a constant multiplicative relationship.

1Which of the following words indicate an decrease?

Select all that apply.

Hint
Which would give a lower value than the original value?
Show answer
Answerdepreciate; discount; loss
WhyDiscount, loss and depreciate all indicate an decrease. A discount is money saved on an item, profit is when you sell something for less money than you paid for it and depreciate is a term used when an item loses its value.
2Which of the following is the correct calculation for finding 5% of 300?
Hint
Rewrite 5% as a multiplier.
Show answer
Answer300 × 0.05
WhyThe decimal equivalent of 5% is 0.05, so 0.05 is the multiplier. The correct calculation is 300 × 0.05
3Match each question to the correct multiplier.
Increase by 4%
Decrease by 40%
Decrease by 6%
Increase by 60%
Hint
Start with 100% and increase or decrease by the percentage and convert this to a multiplier.
Show answer
AnswerIncrease by 4% → ×1.04; Decrease by 40% → ×0.6; Decrease by 6% → ×0.94; Increase by 60% → ×1.6
Why100% + 4% = 104% which is 1.04 as a multiplier. 100% − 40% = 60% which is 0.6 as a multiplier. 100% − 6% = 94% which is 0.94 as a multiplier. 100% + 60% = 160% which is 1.6 as a multiplier.

3The cost of a laptop decreases from £450 to £360. What is the percentage change?
Hint
$$$p$$% of 450 = 360, find $$p$$ and then find the change in percentage.
Show answer
Answer20%
Why$$$p$$ = 360 ÷ 450 × 100 = 80%. 100% − 80% = 20%. So there is a 20% decrease.
4Jun thinks of a number and decreases it by a percentage. The number Jun thought of was 160 and his new number is 136. Jun changed 160 by %

Show answer
Answer15 / 15 % / 15%
Why$$$p$$ = 136 ÷ 160 × 100 = 85%, 100% − 85% = 15%. So, Jun changed his number by 15% (decrease).

Why$$$p$$ = 3726 ÷ 3450 × 100 = 108%. 108% − 100% = 8% increase in Jacob's score.
