Checking understanding of percentages

View on Oak Academy

I can manipulate percentages of an amount without a representation.

Key learning points
  1. Percent relates to the number of parts per hundred.
  2. Percentages can be written as a fraction out of 100.
  3. The equivalence of fractions, decimals and percentages can be used to solve problems.
  4. Percentages of amounts can be calculated by using known fractions.
Keywords
Equivalent fractions
Two fractions are equivalent if they have the same value.

Hint
To find 10%, divide what you know is 100% by 10. Then once you have 10%, divide by 2 to find 5%. 15% = 10% + 5%.
Show answer
Answer10% of 50 → 5; 10% of 80 → 8; 5% of 50 → 2.5; 15% of 50 → 7.5; 15% of 60 → 9; 5% of 60 → 3
WhyFinding 10% by dividing by 10, then halving this to give 5% is a good basis to find any multiple of 10% or 5%. For example, 10% of 60 is 60 ÷ 10 = 6, 5% of 60 is 6 ÷ 2 = 3 and 15% of 60 is 6 + 3 = 9 since 15% = 10% + 5%.
