Equality in an equation

View on Oak Academy

I can appreciate that in an equation the two sides of the equals sign balance.

Key learning points
  1. The equals sign means that the two sides of the equation have the same value.
  2. This can be described as the expressions balancing.
  3. The equality of the expressions can be represented with a bar model.
  4. It is possible to rearrange equations to find the family of alternative versions of the equation.
Keywords
Equation
An equation is used to show two expressions that are equal to each other.

Show answer

WhyThe bottom bar has been multiplied by 3, so multiplying the top bar by 3 will maintain equality. The bottom bar is now $$4x + 10$$ longer so adding $$4x + 10$$ to the top bar will also maintain equality.
