Checking understanding of the mean

View on Oak Academy

I can understand what the mean is measuring, how it is measuring it and calculate the mean from data presented in a list.

Key learning points
  1. The mean can be seen as a redistribution of values such that everything is the same.
  2. The mean is the sum of all values divided by the number of values.
  3. The mean is a measure of central tendency.
Keywords
Central tendency
a summary measure that attempts to describe a whole set of data with a single value that represents the middle of its distribution
