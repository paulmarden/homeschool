Understanding the median

View on Oak Academy

I can understand what the median is measuring, how it is measuring it and find the median from data presented in a list.

Key learning points
  1. The median is a measure of central tendency.
  2. The median is the middle (or average middle) value in an ordered data set.
  3. The median can be seen as the halfway point of a set of values.
Keywords
Median
the central (middle) piece of data when the data is in numerical order. A measure of central tendency representing the average of the values
