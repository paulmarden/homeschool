Comparing statistical representations

View on Oak Academy

I can see that different statistical representations offer different insights into a set of data.

Key learning points
  1. A pie chart and a bar chart offer different insights.
  2. There are certain features of any representation that make it useful.
Keywords
Pie chart
A pie chart (pie graph) is a circular graph where sectors represent different groups proportionally.
Bar chart
A bar chart (bar graph) is a graph consisting of bars visually representing the frequency of objects or groups.

1The bar chart shows the weather over 40 days one winter. What is the modal type of weather? [image]
Hint
Modal means the mode. The mode is the most frequently occurring type of data point in a dataset.
Show answer
Answerwind
WhyWind had the highest frequency of 20, as shown by the tallest bar on the bar chart.
2The bar chart shows the weather over 40 days one winter. How many more days were dry than had hail? days. [image]
Hint
Find the heights of both dry and hail, then perform a subtraction.
Show answer
Answer8 / eight
WhyIt was dry on 8 more days than when it hailed, because 11 − 3 = 8.
3The bar chart shows the weather over 40 days one winter. On what fraction of days did it rain? Give your answer in its simplest form. [image]
Hint
To write this as a fraction, the numerator will include the number of days that it rained. The denominator will include the total number of days represented by the entire bar chart.
Show answer
Answer1/8
WhyIt rained on 5 out of the 40 days. This simplifies to 1/8.
4This pie chart shows 60 vehicles that passed the road nearby a school. What is the modal vehicle that passed nearby the school? [image]
Hint
Modal means the mode. The most frequently occurring type of data point in a data set is also the largest sector of a pie chart.
Show answer
Answercars
WhyThe sector representing cars had a larger angle than the other sectors, therefore cars were the modal vehicle.
5This pie chart shows 60 vehicles that passed the road nearby a school. Which two vehicles were counted an equal number of times? [image]

Select all that apply.

Hint
If two sectors in a pie chart have equal size, then the vehicles those sectors represent are also equal.
Show answer
Answervans; buses
WhyThe sectors for both vans and buses had equal-sized sectors, and therefore their frequency with which both vehicles appeared are also equal.
6This pie chart shows 60 vehicles that passed the road nearby a school. Complete the statement: motorbikes passed nearby the school. [image]
Hint
The sector for motorbikes has an angle of 90°. This represents a quarter of all vehicles that passed the school.
Show answer
Answer15 / fifteen
Why60 vehicles passed in total and the motorbike sector of the pie chart is 90° (which represents a quarter). So a quarter of 60 = 15 motorcycles.

Exit quiz

6 questions
1Match each statement to the graph (bar chart, pie chart, or both) from which the statement can most easily be found. [image]
Lunch was the modal meal
A quarter were breakfast
One more dinner than breakfast
Hint
The mode is the most frequently occurring data point. For a pie chart, this is the largest sector, and for a bar chart, this is the tallest bar.
Show answer
AnswerLunch was the modal meal → both; A quarter were breakfast → pie chart; One more dinner than breakfast → bar chart
WhyFrom a quick snapshot look at the data, it is easier to look at frequencies from a bar chart, and proportions from a pie chart.
2The pie chart and bar chart represent the same data set. Match the bars on the bar chart to the sectors of the pie chart. [image]
a
b
c
Hint
The larger the sector of the pie chart, the taller the equivalent bar will be on the bar chart.
Show answer
Answera → summer; b → autumn; c → spring
WhyThe largest sector in the pie chart is summer so this matches to the tallest bar (a). The smallest sector in the pie chart is spring so this matches to the shortest bar (c).
3The angle of the sector representing autumn on the pie chart is 90°. Using the bar chart, what will the angle of the sector representing spring on the pie chart be? [image]
Hint
The height of the bar representing autumn is 8, whilst the height of the bar representing spring is 4.
Show answer
Answer45°
WhyThe frequency of spring is half the frequency of autumn, therefore half of 90° is 45°.
4The pie chart and bar chart represent the same data set. Which two school trips were equally popular? [image]

Select all that apply.

Hint
Which two sectors of a pie chart have an equal angle? If it is difficult to spot by eye, using a protractor to accurately measure each angle is a sensible option.
Show answer
Answermuseum; beach
WhyBoth museum and beach were equally frequent, with their sectors having an angle of 80°.
5The angle of the sectors for museum and beach are both 80°. What should the height of the bar representing theatre be on the bar chart? [image]
Hint
The angle of the sector representing concert on the pie chart is half the angle for museum.
Show answer
Answer20 / twenty
WhyThe angle of the sector representing museum and beach are 80° and concert is 40°. So the angle of the sector representing theatre is 160°. So the bar representing theatre should be double the height of museum, at 20.
