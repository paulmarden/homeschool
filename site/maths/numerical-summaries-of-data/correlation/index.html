Lesson 14 of 19

Correlation

View on Oak Academy

I can recognise relationships between bivariate data represented on a scatter graph.

Key learning points
  1. The distribution of data points may suggest positive correlation.
  2. The distribution of data points may suggest negative correlation.
  3. The distribution of data points may suggest no correlation.
  4. Correlation does not imply causation
Keywords
Correlation
Correlation describes the extent to which the relationship is close to a linear relationship.
