Diagonals of a rhombus

View on Oak Academy

I can appreciate that the diagonals of a rhombus bisect one another at right angles and the angles are bisected.

Key learning points
  1. An isosceles triangle has a line of symmetry that splits it into two congruent triangles.
  2. In a rhombus each diagonal splits the rhombus into two congruent isosceles triangles bisecting the angles.
  3. The two diagonals meet at right angles.
  4. The two diagonals bisect each other.
Keywords
Bisect
To bisect means to cut or divide an object into two equal parts.
Altitude
The altitude of a triangle is a line segment from a vertex of a triangle to the opposite edge. It is perpendicular to that edge.
