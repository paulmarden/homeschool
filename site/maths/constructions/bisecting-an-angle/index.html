Bisecting an angle

View on Oak Academy

I can use the properties of a rhombus to construct an angle bisector.

Key learning points
  1. A rhombus can be constructed from two congruent isosceles triangles.
  2. The diagonals of a rhombus bisect the angles.
  3. It is possible to construct an angle bisector without drawing the rhombus.
Keywords
Bisect
To bisect means to cut or divide an object into two equal parts.
Rhombus
A rhombus is a parallelogram where all sides are the same length.
Congruent
If one shape can fit exactly on top of another using rotation, reflection or translation, then the shapes are congruent.

2This quadrilateral is a rhombus. What is the size of angle $$r$$? [image]
Hint
152° and $$r$$° are co-interior angles.
Show answer
Answer28 / 28 degrees / 28degrees / 28°
WhyAdjacent angles in a rhombus are always co-interior. Since they share a side of a rhombus that is transversal to two parallel lines, both adjacent angles will always sum to 180°. Therefore, $$r$$ = 180 - 152 = 28°.
