Problem solving with constructions

View on Oak Academy

I can use my knowledge of constructions to solve problems.

Key learning points
  1. Shapes made from triangles can be constructed.
  2. By investigation it is possible to accurately construct multiple polygons.
  3. Shortest distances can be found using constructions with perpendicular lines.
Keywords
Bisect
To bisect means to cut or divide an object into two equal parts.
Rhombus
A rhombus is a parallelogram where all sides are the same length.

Starter quiz

6 questions
1This quadrilateral is a rhombus. Find the value of $$a+b+c$$. [image]
Hint
Angle $$a$$ has the same value as the bisection of angle $$b$$. This means that angle $$b$$ is twice the size of $$a$$.
Show answer
Answer165
Why$$$a$$ = 90 - 65 = 25, and therefore $$b$$ = 50. The angle at the intersection of the diagonals of a rhombus is always 90°. Therefore, the sum of $$a$$, $$b$$, and $$c$$ is 25 + 50 + 90 = 165.
2This triangle is reflected with the line of reflection at its base side. The resultant quadrilateral is a rhombus. What is the area of this rhombus (in cm²)? [image]
Hint
One diagonal of the rhombus will be 22 cm, and the other diagonal will be double the perpendicular height of the original isosceles triangle.
Show answer
Answer2640 / 2640 cm²
WhyThe rhombus will be reflected on the side of length 22 cm. This means that the side length of the rhombus will be 61 cm, and the other diagonal will be of length 2 × 60 = 120 cm.
3This hexagon is regular. Find the size of angle $$v$$, in degrees. [image]
Hint
An interior angle of a regular hexagon is 120°.
Show answer
Answer30°
WhyThis value can be found in several ways. One way is to use the isosceles triangle on the bottom of the hexagon. Its two base angles are both 30°. The bottom left angle in the hexagon is then split into two parts, the 30° and the 120 - 30 = 90° parts.
4The triangle is equilateral, with two angle bisectors extending from the bottom two angles of the equilateral triangle and intersecting inside the triangle. Find the size of angle $$w$$. [image]
Hint
An interior angle of an equilateral triangle is 60°, and therefore its angle bisected is 30°. The triangle that exists inside the equilateral triangle is isosceles.
Show answer
Answer240°
WhyThe isosceles triangle inside the equilateral one has base angles of 30° each. Therefore, the interior angle at the top of the isosceles triangle is 180 - 30 - 30 = 120°. Angles around a point sum to 360°, therefore $$w$$ = 360 - 120 = 240°.
5This diagram shows a kite enclosed by a rectangle. If the rectangle has an area of 780 cm², find the area of the kite, in cm². [image]
Hint
The diagonals of the kite split the larger rectangle up into four smaller rectangles. A part of the kite takes up half the area of each smaller rectangle.
Show answer
Answer390 / 390 cm² / 390cm² / 390 cm squared / 390cm squared
WhyThe area of this kite can be found by halving the product of its two diagonals, as the product of the two diagonals equals the area of the rectangle that encloses it.
6This kite has an area of 270 cm². What is the area of the shaded region: the right-angled triangle on the top, right of the kite? Give your answer in cm². [image]
Hint
If the area of the kite is 270 cm², and the diagonal height is 30 cm, then its diagonal width is 18 cm.
Show answer
Answer27 / 27 cm² / 27cm² / 27 cm squared / 27cm squared
WhyIf the diagonal width of the kite is 18 cm, then the width of the shaded triangle is 9 cm. The height of the shaded triangle is 30 - 24 = 6 cm. Therefore, the area of this shaded triangle is $$1\over2$$ × 6 × 9 = 27 cm².
