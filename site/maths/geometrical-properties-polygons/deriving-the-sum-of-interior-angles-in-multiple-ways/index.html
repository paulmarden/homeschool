Deriving the sum of interior angles in multiple ways

View on Oak Academy

I can use reasoning to derive the sum of interior angles in multiple ways.

Key learning points
  1. Not all vertices of component shapes contribute to the sum of the interior angles of a composite shape.
  2. A numerical sequence can be used to derive the sum of interior angles.
  3. A formula can be derived to find the sum of interior angles.
Keywords
Polygon
A polygon is a flat (2D), closed figure made up of straight line segments.
Interior angle
An interior angle is an angle formed inside a polygon by two of its edges.
Sum
The sum is the total when numbers are added together.
