Checking understanding of angles from KS2

View on Oak Academy

I can calculate with angles at a point, angles on a line and vertically opposite angles.

Key learning points
  1. Angles that meet at a point can be identified in diagrams.
  2. Angles that make a straight line can be identified on a diagram.
  3. Vertically opposite angles can be identified on a diagram.
Keywords
Supplementary anglesSupplementary angles are a pair of angles that sum to 180°.
Conjugate anglesConjugate angles are a pair of angles that sum to 360°.

5Which of these properties of a polygon always remain invariant when the polygon is reflected?

Select all that apply.

Hint
When any polygon is reflected, the size of the object remains invariant, but the location of some of its vertices will change.
Show answer
Answersize of angles; size of sides
WhyEven if a polygon is reflected and its image overlaps the object perfectly, the position of at least some of its vertices will have been reflected. The size of its angles and sides will remain the same (invariant) after a reflection.

WhyAfter an enlargement, the length of the sides of the polygon may change, but the angles both interior and exterior to the polygon will remain the same.
