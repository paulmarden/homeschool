Corresponding angles

View on Oak Academy

I can recognise that a pair of parallel lines traversed by a straight line produces equal corresponding angles.

Key learning points
  1. The different arrangements of 3 line segments can be described and categorised.
  2. One of these arrangements involves a transversal.
  3. Corresponding angles can be identified in this arrangement.
  4. If the lines cut by the transversal are parallel these corresponding angles are equal.
  5. Knowledge of corresponding angles can be used to find unknown angles.
Keywords
Corresponding angles
Corresponding angles are a pair of angles at different vertices on the same side of a transversal in equivalent positions.
Transversal
A transversal is any line that intersects two or more lines at distinct (different) points (line, line segment or ray).
