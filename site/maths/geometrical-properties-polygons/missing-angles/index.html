Missing angles

View on Oak Academy

I can solve problems that require a combination of angles facts to identify values of missing angles, providing explanations of reasoning and logic used.

Key learning points
  1. Missing angles can be found using parallel lines.
  2. Missing angles can be found using facts about triangles.
  3. Missing angles can be found in polygons using knowledge of exterior and interior angles.
  4. The relationship between interior and exterior angles can be used to find missing angles.
  5. Missing angles can be found by using a combination of all known facts.
Keywords
Corresponding angles
A pair of angles at different vertices on the same side of a transversal in equivalent positions.
Alternate angles
A pair of angles both between or both outside two line segments that are on opposite sides of the transversal that cuts them.
Co-interior angles
Co-interior angles are on the same side of the transversal line and in between the two other lines.
Interior angles
An interior angle is an angle formed inside a polygon by two of its edges.
Exterior angle
An exterior angle is an angle on the outside of a polygon between an extension of an edge and its adjacent edge.
