Co-interior angles

View on Oak Academy

I can recognise that a pair of parallel lines traversed by a straight line produces supplementary co-interior angles.

Key learning points
  1. Co-interior angles can be identified in an arrangement of 3 line segments.
  2. If two of the lines are parallel then these angles are supplementary.
  3. Knowledge of co-interior angles can be used to find unknown angles.
  4. Knowledge of co-interior, corresponding and alternate angles can be used to find unknown angles.
Keywords
Co-interior
Co-interior angles are on the same side of the transversal line and in between the two other lines.
