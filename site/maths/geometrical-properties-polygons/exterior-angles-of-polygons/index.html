Exterior angles of polygons

View on Oak Academy

I can find the sum of the exterior angles of a polygon.

Key learning points
  1. The sum of the exterior angles of a polygon can be demonstrated using a point.
  2. The sum of the exterior angles of a polygon can be proven using interior angles and the angles on a straight line.
  3. The angle sum of a triangle can also be proven using exterior angles .
  4. The size of the exterior angle of any polygon can be found using the sum of angles.
Keywords
Interior angle
an angle formed inside a polygon by two of its edges.
Exterior angle
an angle on the outside of a polygon between an extension of an edge and its adjacent edge.
Supplementary angle
a pair of angles that sum to 180°.
