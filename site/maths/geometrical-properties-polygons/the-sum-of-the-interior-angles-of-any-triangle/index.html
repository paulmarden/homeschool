The sum of the interior angles of any triangle

View on Oak Academy

I can demonstrate and prove that in a triangle, the sum of the interior angles is 180°.

Key learning points
  1. By considering a number of different triangles you can demonstrate facts about the angles in triangles.
  2. The interior angles of any triangle sum to 180°
  3. The angle sum of triangles can be proved using angles in parallel lines.
Keywords
Alternate angles
a pair of angles both between or both outside two line segments that are on opposite sides of the transversal that cuts them.
Corresponding angles
Corresponding angles are a pair of angles at different vertices on the same side of a transversal in equivalent positions.
Co-interior angles
Co-interior angles are on the same side of the transversal line and in between the two other lines.
