Using the sum of the interior angles of a triangle

View on Oak Academy

I can use the fact that the interior angles of any triangle sum to 180° to find the size of missing angles.

Key learning points
  1. Missing angles in triangles can be found using the angle sum.
  2. Missing angles in triangles can be found using the angle sum and other triangle properties.
Keywords
Interior angle
an angle formed inside a polygon by two of its edges.
Sum
the total when numbers are added together.
