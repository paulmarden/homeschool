Interior and exterior angles of regular polygons

View on Oak Academy

I can find the interior and exterior angles of regular polygons.

Key learning points
  1. Exterior angles of a regular polygon are equal.
  2. The size of the exterior angle of a regular polygon can be determined.
  3. Interior angles of regular polygons are equal.
  4. The size of the interior angle of a regular polygon can be determined.
  5. The number of sides in a regular polygon can be determined from the size of the interior or exterior angle.
Keywords
Polygon
A polygon is a flat (2D), closed figure made up of straight line segments.
Regular polygon
A regular polygon has sides that are equal and interior (inside) angles that are all equal.
Interior angle
An interior angle is an angle formed inside a polygon by two of its edges.
Exterior angle
An exterior angle is an angle on the outside of a polygon between an extension of an edge and its adjacent edge.
