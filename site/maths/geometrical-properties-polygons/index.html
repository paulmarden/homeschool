Unit 9 of 10

Geometrical properties: polygons

View on Oak Academy

In this unit pupils will investigate internal and external angles of polygons and angles made with a transveral and parallel lines. Pupils will develop their ability to formally prove properties of polygons to generalise.

Geometry and Measure

Why this, why now

This unit builds on knowledge developed investigating angles and continues work done in the previous unit investigating polygons. Here pupils will develop understanding of external and internal angles of polygons and traversals through parallel lines

Prior knowledge
Identify and classify polygons by sides and angles
Use angle rules (interior, exterior)
Calculate sum of angles and solve problems using reasoning
