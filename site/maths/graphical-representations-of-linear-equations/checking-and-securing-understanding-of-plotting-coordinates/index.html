Checking and securing understanding of plotting coordinates

View on Oak Academy

I can plot coordinates in any of the four quadrants.

Key learning points
  1. It is possible to plot coordinates approximately in the correct quadrant.
  2. Coordinates can be plotted exactly when the axes are labelled.
  3. By careful consideration of coordinates the missing vertices of a shape can be found.
Keywords
Quadrant
Any one of the four areas into which a space is divided by the <i>x</i>- and <i>y</i>-axes in the Cartesian coordinate system.
